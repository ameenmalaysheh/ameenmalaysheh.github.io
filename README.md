## About Me

I am **Ameen Khalid Ameen Malaysheh**, an independent interdisciplinary researcher, author, consultant, and developer of theoretical models and frameworks working at the intersection of **scientific inquiry, philosophical thought, practical experience, economic analysis, human studies, and ancient civilizations**.

I approach research as an integrated intellectual process in which experience and research are not separated, and in which practice and analysis inform one another. My methodology is grounded in the view that genuine knowledge emerges through the interaction of **accumulated practical experience, systematic observation, structured analysis, cognitive synthesis, philosophical and ethical reflection, scientific knowledge, and responsible conclusions**.

> **“Research is the outcome of accumulated methodological scientific and practical experience, ethical reflection, structured analysis, cognitive synthesis, careful observation, and responsible conclusion.”**

My professional and research experience extends across **construction and real estate, trade and business, project management, project evaluation, goods and precious-material valuation, and long-term interdisciplinary research in psychology, philosophy of mind, economics, ancient civilizations, and related fields**.

# Philosophy of Work

I believe that knowledge does not belong to a single discipline, and that many human, civilizational, economic, and engineering questions require examination from multiple perspectives simultaneously.

My projects therefore seek to build connections between:

**Human Being ↔ Mind ↔ Society ↔ Economy ↔ Civilization ↔ Engineering ↔ Knowledge**

The purpose of this approach is not simply to combine disciplines, but to investigate structural relationships that may emerge when phenomena are examined through multiple intellectual perspectives.
